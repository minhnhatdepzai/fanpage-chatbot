TASK 1 COACH — SOURCES AND THIRD-PARTY NOTICES

Original curriculum
-------------------
The existing vocabulary, grammar, paraphrase, samples and original prompts
come from https://github.com/Thanh-Fourteen/task1-coach. Their original
source fields are retained. No claim of Cambridge/IELTS endorsement is made.
The extra 20 writing prompts and 40 exercises are original practice content
with simulated data, not past examination papers.

Listening practice and generated narration (2026-09-17)
-----------------------------------------------------
Thirty original shortened scripts, 300 questions and Vietnamese explanations.
These are not official IELTS/Cambridge papers or a full timed mock test.
MP3 narration is synthetic, generated locally using Piper 1.8.0 / Alba medium.
No voice model or speech engine is shipped. Dataset credit: Cassia
Valentini-Botinhao and Junichi Yamagishi, University of Edinburgh (2019),
English multi-speaker corpus for CSTR voice cloning toolkit (version 0.92),
CC BY 4.0, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2506
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Full provenance, changes and model-card links: /listening/README.md
Synthetic output is not a recording of a real interview or an endorsement.

Reading adaptations (reviewed 2026-09-17)
---------------------------------------
The additional 26 Reading scenarios are wholly original fictional teaching
case studies, not Wikipedia adaptations or accounts of actual research.
See /practice-sources.md. The following credits apply to the original four passages.
Wikipedia contributors: Urban heat island, Biomimetics, Citizen science.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_heat_island
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomimetics
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizen_science
Each reading passage is a shortened, simplified adaptation, not a verbatim
article. The adaptations are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Author histories are linked in each lesson. Questions and Vietnamese
explanations are newly authored. This content license does not relicense
the application code or unrelated assets.

OpenWiki README: https://github.com/langchain-ai/openwiki (MIT).
Adapted into a short reading about source-grounded documentation.
OpenWiki itself is not installed, run or connected to learner data.
