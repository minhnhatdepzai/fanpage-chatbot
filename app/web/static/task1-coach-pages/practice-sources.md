# Original practice bank

The additional Reading case studies are fictional teaching scenarios written for
Task 1 Coach. They do not describe actual experiments, institutions, measured
results or peer-reviewed research. Use only the passage to answer its questions.
They practise short-form reading comprehension, not the length or difficulty of
a complete official IELTS paper. No Cambridge text is reproduced here.

The original four source-adapted passages retain their individual source and
license notices. Listening scripts, grammar drills and Speaking topics in the
new bank are original teaching material. Synthetic narration is labelled in the
player. Speaking self-assessment is not an official IELTS score or AI evaluation.
